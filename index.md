## 1. Game Overview & Instructions

### Game Rules
* **Circular Playfield**: The game board is a 3D cylinder with a grid size of **18 × 10**. Each horizontal row consists of a ring of 18 blocks, with a maximum height of 10 blocks.
* **Falling Blocks**: Each falling piece (tetromino) is made up of 4 individual blocks.
* **Line Clearance**: When you successfully fill an entire horizontal ring of 18 blocks, that row is cleared, and points are awarded.
* **Victory Condition**: Safely place and complete **100 components** to pass the level.
* **Game Over**: The game ends if the stacked blocks reach the top of the 10-row grid and no new pieces can be placed.

### Controls
The game features a classic virtual button layout. Five semi-transparent buttons are evenly distributed across the bottom of the screen:
1. **Rotate World Left (<)**: Rotates the entire game camera/field to the left to view the stacking from different angles.
2. **Rotate World Right (>)**: Rotates the game camera/field to the right.
3. **Hold**: Temporarily stores the current falling block, or swaps it with a previously stored one.
4. **Rotate Block (🔄)**: Changes the orientation and shape of the falling piece.
5. **Drop (⬇️)**: Accelerates the descent of the falling block (soft drop or hard drop).

### Camera Adjustments
* **Pause Menu**: Tap the **center area of the screen** to pause the game and access the pause menu (Resume / Restart).
* **Distance Slider**: Adjusts the size of the playfield on your screen (zoom in or zoom out).
* **V-Shift Slider**: Adjusts the vertical tilt/height of the camera to help you better observe the falling blocks.

### Troubleshooting (FAQ)
1. **What should I do if the game runs slowly or stutters?**
   * Try closing other background apps on your iOS device or restarting the device.
2. **The playfield is too small or hard to see?**
   * Use the `Distance` and `V-Shift` sliders on the left and right sides of the screen to adjust the camera perspective to a comfortable view.
3. **There is no sound in the game?**
   * Ensure that your iOS device is not set to silent mode and that the media volume is turned up.
